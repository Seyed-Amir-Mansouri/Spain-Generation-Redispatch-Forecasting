Grid redispatch forecast

Spain

Every day, Red Eléctrica (REE), Spain's transmission system operator, redispatches generating units to solve technical constraints on the grid: it raises some units above their day-ahead schedule and lowers others. This tool forecasts, for 41 large generating units and every hour of the day, whether REE will redispatch them upward, downward or not at all.
