Grid redispatch forecast

Spain

Every day, Spain's grid operator, Red Eléctrica (REE), checks the electricity market's production plan against what the grid can safely carry. Where it cannot, REE orders some power plants to produce more and others to produce less. This is called redispatch. Our model forecasts these orders for 41 large generating units from public data. This page compares its forecasts with what REE actually did over 14 past days.

For every unit and hour, the model gives a chance from 0 to 1 that REE will move it. A unit is called (forecast to be moved) when its chance reaches this threshold. Slide right to be stricter: fewer calls, more of them right, but more real moves missed.
