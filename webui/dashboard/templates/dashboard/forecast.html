Pick the delivery days

Choose up to 14 delivery days, anywhere from 1 January 2026 up to tomorrow. Data up to 17 July 2026 is already on disk; later days are downloaded when you ask for them. A day's forecast uses data published before and on that day: the SDAC price and forecasts, REE's day-ahead schedule and National Phase 1 redispatch, the intraday auctions (IDA3 clears on the delivery day itself), the weather forecast, and each unit's actual output on the two days before.

Data needed

The data a forecast uses

NameData in itSourcePublished (Madrid time)Why it is used
Single Day-Ahead Coupling (SDAC) Day-ahead price for Spain; Spain's demand, wind and solar forecasts for every hour of the delivery day. ENTSO-E Prices about 13:00 the day before; forecasts by 18:00 the day before. The European auction that sets the delivery day's prices. Low prices with high wind and solar often mean renewables are moved down; low demand with little gas often means gas units are started for voltage.
Intraday auctions IDA1, IDA2 and IDA3 prices, and each one's difference from the day-ahead price. OMIE IDA1 clears 15:00 and IDA2 22:00 the day before; IDA3 clears 10:00 on the delivery day, for hours 13 to 24 only. OMIE's files can appear later. Later auctions where generators adjust their positions; they show how the schedule shifted after SDAC.
National Phase 1 redispatch Energy REE moves up and down for each reason: overload in normal operation, overload after a fault, voltage, distribution grid, security and other, plus the totals. REE e-sios Afternoon of the day before. REE's own peninsula-wide figure for how much it redispatches to fix the grid. The model still has to work out which units are moved.
Day-ahead schedule Scheduled energy per technology: combined cycle, nuclear, wind, solar PV, solar thermal, hydro, pumped storage, cogeneration, and imports and exports with France and Portugal. REE e-sios Early afternoon of the day before. The base schedule (PBF) that REE checks in Phase 1. When little gas or nuclear is scheduled, REE usually adds gas units to keep the grid stable.
Weather forecast Wind speed at 100 m, solar radiation and cloud cover, averaged over 2° areas around the wind and solar sites. Open-Meteo Days ahead, updated through the day. Wind and sun near the plants decide where renewable output meets grid limits. Area averages worked better in tests than single sites.
Unit output Actual output of each of the 41 units and their sister units at the same plant, for two days before delivery and the day before up to noon. ENTSO-E About an hour after real time, but often a day or more late. REE tends to move the units it moved on recent days, and often takes turns between sister units. Two days before is the newest full day available at noon the day before.

Each marker is the time a source normally publishes that input. The thin grey line runs through the whole period; the thicker grey bar is the period the data describes: the delivery day for market, grid and weather data, and the days before for the units' actual output. A forecast can only be issued once every marker is green.
